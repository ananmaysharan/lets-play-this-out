Paper Review: Let's Play This Out: The Future of AI and Work

Targeted at the current ACM sigconf draft. Severity: HIGH blocks coherence or breaks compilation, MED inconsistency a reader will notice, LOW polish.

Top-line read

The substantive argument is there. The two things making it feel assembled are (1) the citation system is in three different styles at once and (2) the section structure has the flow of an evolving outline, not a paper. The Introduction restates the Background, the System Building section is written in future tense even though playtest data already exists, and the new Playtest section is buried as a subsection under "Methods, Concepts and Motivations" instead of being its own Findings section. There is no Discussion or Conclusion, and the appendix promises a diagram that isn't there.

Most of this is fixable in a single editing pass. The fastest unlocks are: pick one citation style and apply it everywhere, hoist Playtest Observations to a top-level section, and add a short Discussion / Conclusion.

Specific structural issues

  • FAQ-style subsections in Introduction: \subsection{What is the problem?} and \subsection{Why is it important?} read like an undergraduate brief. Most ACM HCI papers use a single Introduction with no subsections. Fold these into 3 flowing paragraphs.
  • The four-futures taxonomy (Augmentation, Job Erosion, Selective Shrinkage, Pro-Worker Redesign) currently sits inside "Methods, Concepts and Motivations." It is the conceptual core of the design — it should live at the top of the Design Rationale section.
  • Playtest is a subsection under "Methods", which doesn't make sense. Empirical observations are a Findings/Results section in their own right.
  • No Discussion or Conclusion. The paper ends with playtest numbers, then jumps straight to Acknowledgments. Add at least a short Discussion (limitations, what we'd change, what this hints at for the evaluation framework you describe in §4.1) and a one-paragraph Conclusion.
  • Empty appendix: the prose says "Please see the graphic at the end of this document for a visual gameplay flow diagram" but \appendix is empty. Either add the figure or remove the sentence.
